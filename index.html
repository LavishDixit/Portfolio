What I actually spend my time on

I'm a pre-final year Computer Science Engineering student at VIT Bhopal University, specializing in AI & Machine Learning. Most of what I build comes back to the same question: how do you get a system to find the right piece of information out of thousands, fast enough that nobody notices the search happening at all.

That question is why I've spent the last year deep in retrieval — RAG pipelines, semantic caching, vector search with FAISS, and squeezing latency out of LLM applications running on the Groq API. I like backend work that's invisible when it's done right: a Chrome extension that summarizes a page before you finish reading the headline, a search engine that remembers what it's already answered so it never does the same work twice.

I'm currently looking for an AI/ML or AI Engineer internship where I can take that habit — building things that quietly work — and put it against problems that actually ship.
